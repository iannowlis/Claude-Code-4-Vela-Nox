CANCELLATION POLICY

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Your Vela Nox subscription costs $3 per week, billed weekly through Stripe. It renews automatically every week until you cancel.

How to cancel

You can cancel at any time by using the "manage subscription" link in your Stripe billing emails, or by emailing velanox@gmail.com from the email address you subscribed with. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current paid week. You will not be charged again after that.

After you cancel

You keep full access until the end of the week you have already paid for, including submitting a sound for that week and receiving that week's early-access email if it falls within your paid period. After that, access to the Submit page ends and you will be removed from Vela's Close Friends list.

Refunds

Payments are non-refundable, and we do not offer refunds or credits for partial weeks, unused weeks, or weeks in which your sound was not selected, except where required by law.

If you believe you were charged by mistake (for example, after cancelling, or charged twice), email velanox@gmail.com within 14 days of the charge and we will look into it and refund any charge made in error.

Sounds you have already submitted

Cancelling does not remove sounds you have already submitted from tracks that have been released or are in production. Your credits on released tracks stay in place. You can ask to be removed from the Signal Map and public website credits at any time by emailing velanox@gmail.com.

Price changes

If the price ever changes, we will tell you by email at least 14 days before the new price applies. You can cancel before then if you do not want to continue.
